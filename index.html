ManifestoManifesto

Elas foram veladas à luz de vela, em quatro caixões lado a lado. Ninguém esperava que o silêncio pegasse fogo. Do próprio enterro fizeram um show: o caos virou ritmo, as cinzas viraram coroa, e o mundo frio foi tingido de vermelho. They were laid to rest by candlelight, four coffins side by side. No one expected the silence to catch fire. They turned their own funeral into a show: chaos became rhythm, ashes became a crown, and a cold world was dyed red.
